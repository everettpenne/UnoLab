# Uno Lab 0.3.7

The assistant gesture works again. 0.3.6 aimed it at Uno Launcher's floating search window, which crashed the launcher.

## What's fixed

Uno Launcher 0.132.3 and 0.132.4 added a translucent activity meant to show search over the app instead of taking you Home. Two problems:

1. Its panel focused its text field before Compose had placed it, throwing `Expected BringIntoViewRequester to not be used before parents are placed` and killing the launcher. (Fixed in the launcher by not taking focus automatically — a tap focuses it.)
2. With that fixed, the panel **painted nothing** in a standalone activity, even though the window itself drew.

So the gesture goes back to Home's search, which works, while the floating window is sorted out. Nothing else changes: the panel still opens over Home, the answer still arrives, the island still reports its state.
